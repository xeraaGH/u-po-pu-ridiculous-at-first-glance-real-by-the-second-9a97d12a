# Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second

Entry for the make.fun bounty [Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-ridiculous-at-first-glance-real-by-the-second-9a97d12a): a single sentence pitching a bizarre startup idea that sounds absurd until you realise it would actually sell.

The program (`main.ts`, Node 22) writes the pitch deterministically to `/output/entry.txt`. The pitch: professional mourners hired to cry at your own funeral rehearsal, so you can watch who actually shows up, hear what they would say, and rewrite your will before it is too late — a funeral rehearsal service for people who want to audit their legacy while they can still change it.
